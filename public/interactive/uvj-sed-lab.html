About this explorer · methods & limitations

Reference spectra. The 16 starting SEDs are reconstructions of the binned FAST/BC03 models in Suess et al. (2021), Figure 2. They are interpolated in log λ and log Fλ over 1500–30,000 Å, with no extrapolation. Hollow UVJ points show the published group colors from Figure 3; the gold point shows colors calculated from the current spectrum.

Galaxy images. The thumbnails are observed examples from the selected reference group in Suess et al. (2021), Figure 4, using HST/CANDELS F160W, F125W and F814W imaging. Each cutout spans 3″ × 3″ (approximately 25 kpc across). The two examples share a stellar-mass bin in different redshift intervals; log M★ labels are approximate bin centers in solar-mass units. These are unaltered photographs, not predictions for the modified SED, and the sliders do not change them.

Shape controls. Each reference is normalized at 5500 Å and mixed linearly in Fλ with the blue star-forming template G1. Extra dust applies A(λ) = ΔAV(λ/5500 Å)−0.7, followed by renormalization. Below 3000 Å, the UV slope control multiplies the continuum by (λ/3000 Å)Δβ. It leaves UVJ unchanged because these bands sample longer wavelengths.

Photometry. Colors use photon-counting AB averages through the Maíz Apellániz Johnson U and V and 2MASS J responses (EAZY filter tables 153, 155 and 161):

⟨Fν⟩ = ∫ Fλ(λ) T(λ) λ dλ / [c ∫ T(λ) dλ/λ]
U−V = −2.5 log₁₀(⟨Fν⟩U / ⟨Fν⟩V)
V−J = −2.5 log₁₀(⟨Fν⟩V / ⟨Fν⟩J)

Spectral units. Fλ is flux density per unit wavelength; Fν is per unit frequency, with Fν = λ²Fλ / c. Switching between them changes the spectrum’s appearance, not its colors.

Quiescent boundary. U−V > 1.3, V−J < 1.5, and U−V > 0.8(V−J) + 0.7, following Figure 3 and its cited Whitaker et al. (2012) convention. “Near boundary” indicates a point within 0.03 mag of an edge; it is not an uncertainty estimate.

Matching colors. Moving the gold point searches the 16 references, blue-light fractions 0–1 and extra ΔAV = 0–1.5 for a nearby solution, keeping the UV slope fixed. A cross marks colors outside the available model range. Two colors do not uniquely determine an SED, and moving through UVJ is not an evolutionary track.

Limitations. This is an educational model, not a stellar-population fitting tool. Binning, digitization, interpolation and filter choices cause differences from the published colors, especially for G1’s sparsely sampled near-infrared spectrum. The reference fits use BC03, a Chabrier IMF, a Kriek & Conroy attenuation law and delayed-exponential histories. Their Table 1 ages, attenuation and sSFR remain reference properties, not estimates for modified spectra. No nebular emission is included, and the broad break is not a measured Dn4000 index.
